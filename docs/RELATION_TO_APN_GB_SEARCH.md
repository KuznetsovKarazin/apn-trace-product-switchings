# Relation to `apn-gb-search`

The repositories represent different research objects.

## `apn-gb-search`

- Historical Gröbner-basis discovery and full-slice campaign.
- Depends on Magma, SageMath/sboxU, large intermediate data, and external APN databases.
- The published v1-era claims about four new CCZ classes are obsolete and must be corrected.
- Its durable contribution is the search pipeline and the observed local slice geometry.

## `apn-trace-product-switchings`

- Theoretical companion for low-rank trace-product switchings.
- Exact Python certificates; no broad Gröbner campaign is needed to reproduce the final theorems.
- Main claims: dimension classification, uniform nonexistence above dimension eight, and projective rank-two geometry.

A branch of the old repository would obscure this separation and would tie the new paper to obsolete release metadata. A new repository is therefore recommended.
